About the developer

Jankal Group

A family-owned name in construction for decades — and the group behind Stellar Heights.

Building For Eternity

Every structure we build is a monument to the power of human imagination and hard work.

Jankal Group is a distinguished, family-owned enterprise with a longstanding presence in the construction industry, spanning several decades.

We steward our firm with the honour of our family name at its heart, striving each day to elevate it through the quality of every project we undertake.

We build spaces that connect people and strengthen community. Our residential projects are meticulously designed to seamlessly integrate comfort, community, and creativity, forging vibrant neighbourhoods that instil a profound sense of belonging.

Together, we are not simply constructing houses; we are fashioning a lifestyle that exalts the beauty of living well. At Jankal Group, we firmly believe that home is more than just a place — it's a sanctuary where life unfolds.

Our vision

Homes That Outlast Generations

To be Bengaluru's most trusted name in residential development — known for homes that are built to last, planned around real family life, and set in neighbourhoods people are proud to belong to.
