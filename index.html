Building For Eternity

Specifications

What goes into every home and every common area. Open any line for the detail.

Structure

Basement + Stilt + 10 floors, RCC framed structure. High-quality solid concrete block masonry.

Plastering

All internal walls will be smoothly plastered to give an even finish; external two-coat plastering with a sponge finish.

Flooring

Foyer, living, dining, family room, kitchen and bedrooms:Vitrified tile flooring.

Utility balcony and private terrace:Anti-skid ceramic tile flooring.

Toilets:Ceramic tile flooring and glazed ceramic tile cladding up to the false ceiling.

Ground floor entrance lobby, staircase, and common area:Anti-skid tiles.

Doors

Main door:8-foot engineered doors (termite and moisture proof). Good quality brass/chrome finished hardware. Features My Gate integrated smart locks.

Room and toilet doors:7-foot engineered doors (termite and moisture proof). Good quality brass/chrome finished hardware.

Windows

3-track UPVC architectural window system and glass panels with mosquito mesh.

Kitchen

Provision for a plumbing point for a sink and an electrical point to accommodate a modular kitchen.

Provision for the water purifier point.

Provision for a washing machine point in the utility area.

Plumbing

All plumbing lines are pressure tested. All water supply lines are of C.P.V.C. of reputed make. PVC sewer lines. Grohe Fittings & Water Softener.

Painting

2 coats of interior paints. Distemper for ceilings.

Exterior:Exterior emulsion paint.

Electrical & Power Backup

Concealed conduit with fire-retardant low smoke (FRLS) electrical wires.

Earth leakage protection at the MCB distribution at each apartment.

Legrand brand or equivalent.

AC provision for all rooms, geyser provision, and exhaust provision for all toilets.

Power backup100% standby generator for common areas. 1 kW backup power for each flat.

Lift

4 lifts from a reputed brand.

Fire Fighting System

Sprinkler system for common areas & firefighting stairways. Fire hose and extinguisher on every floor.

Sustainable

Rainwater harvesting and organic waste converter.
